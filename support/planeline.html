Why is a plane missing?

Positions come from volunteer ADS-B receivers. Planes outside their coverage, or not broadcasting ADS-B, can't be shown.

Why does it say "Route unconfirmed"?

Routes come from crowd-sourced data. Planeline only shows a route as fact when the plane's actual path matches it.

Can I use it for navigation?

No. Planeline is for interest only. Data can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. It is not affiliated with any airline, airport, air navigation service or other flight tracker.
