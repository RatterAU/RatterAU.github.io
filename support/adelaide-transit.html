The first launch is downloading something

The full Adelaide Metro timetable (about 20 MB) is downloaded once so departures work quickly and offline. After that it only updates when Adelaide Metro publishes a new timetable, on Wi-Fi unless you allow cellular in Settings.

A bus isn't on the map

Only vehicles currently reporting GPS to Adelaide Metro appear. Scheduled trips without a live vehicle show their timetabled time.

Is this the official Adelaide Metro app?

No. Adelaide Transit is an independent app, not affiliated with Adelaide Metro, the Department for Infrastructure and Transport or the Government of South Australia. Data © Department for Infrastructure and Transport, South Australia, used under CC BY 4.0.
