Planeline Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Summary

Planeline doesn't collect any personal data. The developer runs no servers that receive anything from the app: no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.

Location

If you allow it, your location is used on your phone, while the app is open, to centre the map and work out which planes are near you. It is never stored off your phone or sent to the developer.

Network requests

Planeline asks public data services directly for what's on screen: aircraft positions for the area of the map you are viewing (adsb.lol, adsb.fi), weather for that area (aviationweather.gov, RainViewer, Open-Meteo, NASA), and details, routes, past flights and photos of a plane you tap (adsbdb, adsb.lol, OpenSky, GitHub Pages, Wikimedia Commons). Map tiles come from Apple. Because the map is often centred on you, the area requested can be near where you are. Like any internet request, these services see your IP address. Nothing identifying you is added to the requests, and none of them is told who you are.

Stored on your phone

Your settings and some downloaded data (cached aircraft details and map layers) are kept on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new date.
