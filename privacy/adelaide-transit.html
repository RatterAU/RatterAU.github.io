Adelaide Transit Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Summary

Adelaide Transit doesn't collect any personal data. The developer runs no servers and receives nothing from the app: no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.

Location

If you allow it, your location is used only on your phone, while the app is open, to centre the map, sort stops by distance and estimate walking times. It is never stored off your phone or sent to the developer. When you ask for walking times, the start and end points are sent to Apple Maps to work out the route, under Apple's privacy policy.

Network requests

The app downloads timetable, real-time and alert data directly from Adelaide Metro's public servers, and map tiles from Apple. Like any internet request, those servers see your IP address. Nothing identifying you is added to the requests.

Stored on your phone

Your favourite stops and routes, your settings and the downloaded timetable are kept on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new date.
